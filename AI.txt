1. 🤖 Virtual Beauty & Fragrance Consultant (AI Chatbot)
Given the rich lore around ingredients like Saffron, Oud, and Sandalwood, a standard FAQ bot won't cut it. You can build a custom AI consultant using Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) (via OpenAI or Gemini API).

How it works: You feed your product catalog (skin types, scent families, ingredient stories) into the AI.
Customer Experience: Users can ask, "I have dry skin and love woody scents. What do you recommend?" The AI will parse this, retrieve matching products, and respond with the brand's luxurious tone, guiding them directly to checkout.

2. ✍️ AI-Generated "Story-Driven" Product Descriptions (Admin Portal)
Luxury e-commerce relies heavily on evocative copywriting. You can add an "Auto-Generate Description" button in your Add Product Wizard.

How it works: The admin selects the category, tags, scent family, and key ingredients. The backend sends a prompt to an LLM to generate a rich, storytelling-heavy product description formatted perfectly for the storefront.
Benefit: Saves hours of copywriting time while maintaining a consistent, premium brand voice.

3. 🔍 Smart Review Analysis & Auto-Moderation (Admin Portal)
Since you already have a Review Moderation panel, AI can make it much more powerful.

Sentiment Analysis: Automatically tag incoming reviews as Positive, Neutral, or Negative.
Review Summarization: If a product gets 50+ reviews, the AI can generate a one-paragraph summary for the admin dashboard (e.g., "Customers love the scent of the Oud perfume, but 15% mentioned the pump mechanism is slightly stiff.").
Auto-Moderation: Automatically flag or hide reviews that contain profanity or spam before an admin even has to read them.
4. 🎯 Personalized AI Product Recommendations (Storefront)
Instead of a static "Related Products" section, implement AI-driven recommendations based on user behavior and profiles.

How it works: If a logged-in user frequently buys Anti-aging products or has Sensitive skin in their profile, the AI ranks the homepage "Featured" and "Discover" sections to prioritize those specific items.
Vector Search: You can use MongoDB Atlas Vector Search to find products that are semantically similar based on their descriptions and tags.
5. 📸 AI Skin Analysis (Advanced Frontend Feature)
For the ultimate luxury experience, integrate a computer vision API.

How it works: Customers upload a selfie in the "Skin Care" section. The AI analyzes the image to detect their skin type (oily, dry, combination) or concerns (redness, fine lines).
Result: The system automatically filters the product catalog to show only the skincare formulations that perfectly match their AI-diagnosed skin profile.
